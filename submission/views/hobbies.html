Volunteering

I am passionate about giving back to the community and have actively participated in organizations such as Youth Roots, as well as annual outreach efforts with my church’s youth group to feed the homeless in Denver and support local food banks and shelters. These experiences have deepened my sense of empathy and strengthened my commitment to service and community support.

Sports

Sports have been a lifelong interest of mine, both as a player and as a fan. I enjoy watching basketball and football and play basketball regularly a few times a week to stay active. Engaging in sports has helped me develop discipline, teamwork, and a strong sense of perseverance, traits that I carry into my academic and professional life.

Games/Video Games

Gaming has not only been a source of enjoyment and stress relief, but also a key influence in my decision to pursue a degree in computer science. Through multiplayer gaming, I have developed valuable communication, collaboration, and strategic thinking skills that continue to support my growth as a student and future professional.
